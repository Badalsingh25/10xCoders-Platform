- **Employability**: Shifts focus from "watching tutorials" to "building skills," directly increasing job readiness.

**2. Business Model (Sustainability)**
- **Freemium B2C**: Free basic access + Premium subscription for Unlimited AI Interviews & Verified Certificates.

**3. Scalability (Future Ready)**
